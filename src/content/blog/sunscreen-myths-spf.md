Sunscreen is the simplest piece of skin innovation most of us ignore.

## SPF is not a time limit

SPF measures protection against UVB. Higher numbers help only slightly, and **how much you apply and how often you reapply** matters far more.

## A quick routine

- Apply generously, about two finger lengths for the face and neck
- Reapply every two hours outdoors
- Pair it with shade and clothing
